Every plushie starts as a drawing. This one began as a quick sketch of a fox curled up asleep, and it slowly turned into a pattern.

## Knitting the body

The body is knitted in the round, starting at the base and decreasing toward the neck. The head is knitted separately and stitched on, which makes it easier to get the tilt just right.

## The tail

The tail took three attempts. The trick in the end was brushing the yarn after knitting to make it fluffy.
