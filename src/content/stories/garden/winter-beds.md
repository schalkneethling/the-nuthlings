The summer beds are tired. The tomatoes have given everything they had, and the beans are more stem than pod. It is time to clear them and think about what will carry us through the colder months.

## What is going in

Kale and broad beans do well here, even with the wind. Garlic goes in now and comes out at the start of summer. We collect seaweed from the beach after a storm, rinse it, and spread it over the beds as mulch.

## Keeping the wind off

A row of old pallets along the sea-facing side of the beds makes a surprising difference. The plants behind it grow straighter and the soil stays damp for longer.
