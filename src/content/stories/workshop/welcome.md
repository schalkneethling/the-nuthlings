This is our family's corner of the internet. It is a place for the things we make, grow, cook, draw, and play, and for the animals who keep us company while we do it.

Most of what lives here is not mine. It belongs to everyone else in the house, and the website is set up so that each of us has a place to put our work.

## How the site is organized

Every part of the site is a place on the holding. The garden has its own page, so does the kitchen, the craft room, the studio, the music shed, the yard where the animals live, and the bench by the sea where we write about living with type 2 diabetes. My workshop is where the computers live.

## Adding something new

Each story is a small text file. Write it, add a title and a date at the top, and it shows up in the right place. If writing the file is a hassle, tell me about it over dinner and I will put it up for you.
