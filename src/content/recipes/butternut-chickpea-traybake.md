---
title: Butternut and chickpea traybake
description: One tray, a handful of garden vegetables, and very little washing up.
date: 2026-10-10
author: mom
serves: 4
prepMinutes: 15
cookMinutes: 40
carbsPerServing: 34
ingredients:
  - 600 g butternut squash, peeled and cut into 2 cm cubes
  - 1 can (400 g) chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - 1 red onion, cut into wedges
  - 2 tbsp olive oil
  - 1 tsp smoked paprika
  - 1 tsp ground cumin
  - 100 g feta, crumbled
  - A handful of parsley from the garden, chopped
  - Salt and black pepper
tags: [vegetarian, one-tray, carb-aware]
sample: true
---

1. Heat the oven to 200 °C (390 °F).
2. Toss the squash, chickpeas, and onion with the oil, paprika, and cumin. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Spread everything out on a large tray in a single layer. Crowding the tray steams the vegetables instead of roasting them.
4. Roast for 35 to 40 minutes, turning once halfway, until the squash is soft and catching at the edges.
5. Scatter the feta and parsley over the top and serve straight from the tray.

The carbohydrate figure is a rough estimate per serving. This is a sample recipe. Replace it with one of the family favorites.
